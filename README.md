# MacbookAnimation
Hello I am 13 years old I made a Mac Book Animation the goal for me was to rereate an apple commericial but make it my own by adding my own scenes with my own ideas while keeping the same style that apple does. I also wanted it to be good enough that maybe it could pass as an apple commericial, I also wanted to show them but they have a policy where they can't look at fan art but its okay because I am sharing it to you guys. I made this using blender and the text and some of the transitions where added on after using an editing software called capcut. It took me about 12 hours to make this and I wanted to say that I learned a lot by doing these this was my first time ever creating an animation this good I am happy and suprised with my final result. I hope you guys really enjoy it (BTW if you cant view it in the github repo just use the demo URL)!!!
